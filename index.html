Hi there! I'm Fran, a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at UNSW Sydney. My research focuses on the intersection between artificial intelligence and cognitive science. I am particularly interested in the use of computational models to reproduce (and thus understand) human development and learning.

I am part of the working with Dr Francisco Cruz on developmentally inspired reinforcement learning for robotics. Previously, I did a PhD in Physics at the Frankfurt Institute for Advanced Studies, Germany, with Prof Jochen Triesch, a MSc. in Cognitive Science at the Universidad de la República, Uruguay, with Prof Andrés Pomi, and a BSc. in Physics at the Universidad de la República, Uruguay.
